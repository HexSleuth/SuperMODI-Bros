3D mode builds the entire level, player, enemies, coins and items as real 3D geometry with Three.js. The camera auto-detects screen aspect ratio, player speed, jump velocity and level bounds, then auto-adjusts its angle, distance and height every frame. Inspired by the 3D Object Viewer (Super Mario Bros.) project by Rúben André Barreiro.
